Our Philosophy

The right table,
every single time.

Best Western Premier Chateau Granville's dining guide was born from a simple belief: finding an exceptional meal in an unfamiliar city shouldn't require hours of conflicting reviews or expensive guesswork.

We've hand-selected Vancouver's finest restaurants — spanning intimate fine dining rooms, soulful ramen dens, and beloved weekend brunch spots — and mapped them so you can find exactly what you're craving, exactly where you are.

Whether you're a hotel guest seeking a spontaneous dinner reservation or a visitor planning a full culinary evening, our curated map places the city's best within reach — sorted by occasion, filtered by mood.
